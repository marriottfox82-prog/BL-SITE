Privacy & data

Bandwolf stores your name, instrument role, email address, and optional phone number. This information is shared only with members of your band. It is never sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties.

Email and phone are used solely for band contact and account recovery. You can permanently delete your account and all data at any time from within the app via Settings → Delete account.

What we collect and why:

    We do not use analytics tracking, advertising SDKs, or any form of data monetisation. Your band data stays between you and your bandmates.
